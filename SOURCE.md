## Research context

The [Cambridge record](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/54dabeea-836d-4752-be7f-1d68d43b8568) describes the native EDX data as requiring proprietary AZtec software. The associated [paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11085-023-10218-7) studies Nb, Ta and Ti effects on oxidation of Ni-based superalloys; its bulk-composition table is not a direct reference for these post-oxidation map-sum spectra.

[RosettaSciIO issue 97](https://github.com/hyperspy/rosettasciio/issues/97) tracks overlapping native-converter work. This repository contributes recovery of the licensed Cambridge dataset and a validation fixture, without claiming priority or universal-reader support.

The [example visualization](recovered/preview.png) was generated by `reanalyse.py`. Each panel uses its own 99th-percentile display ceiling; element labels indicate approximate energy windows, not quantified compositions.
